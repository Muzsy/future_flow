## Chapter 3 — Room for an Answer

What contact with your long-standing friend could remain affordable? You separate the impressions from your completed visit from the information you still need. The visit belongs on the page, but it cannot answer everything simply because you went.

There is a difference between imagining an ordinary week elsewhere and knowing what would make that week possible. Your revised worksheet gives that difference room. No destination is settled, no rental offer waits behind the housing question, and no work arrangement makes the employment question disappear. What you have is a clearer way to look.

Leaving room for that answer keeps the friendship from becoming another item you have scheduled into place. The draft remains unsent, its possibilities still conditional.
