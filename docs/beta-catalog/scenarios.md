# Five English starting Scenarios

[Reading catalog](README.md)

These are fictional starting situations for a separate private life. Official Scenario status approves the template; it does not approve any subsequently generated personal story. Personalization and spending remain separately gated.

## A new skill, without a clean break

A steady administrative job pays the bills, but learning something new may open a different working life. The challenge is finding time before making a major commitment.

Goal: Develop practical data-analysis skills and test whether a career change fits.

Motivation: Have work that feels more engaging without gambling away stability.

Starting situation: 35–44 age band, employed, US. Work area: Office administration.

Fictional finances: USD 4200 liquid funds; USD 2600 monthly income. Available time: 8 hours per week.

Constraint (soft): Prefer to keep a modest financial buffer while making progress.

## The first small service

A practical repair skill could become a small weekend service. There are no customers or business assets yet, and limited spare time makes the first commitment matter.

Goal: Test a small bicycle-repair service alongside a regular job.

Motivation: Learn whether independent work can be useful and sustainable.

Starting situation: 35–44 age band, employed, US. Work area: Customer support.

Fictional finances: USD 3600 liquid funds; USD 2600 monthly income. Available time: 6 hours per week.

Constraint (soft): Prefer to keep a modest financial buffer while making progress.

## Room to breathe again

A period of unexpected expenses has left little cash in reserve. Regular work continues, but rebuilding a buffer competes with time, fatigue and the wish for an ordinary social life.

Goal: Rebuild a manageable financial buffer through realistic spending and work choices.

Motivation: Feel less exposed when an ordinary unexpected expense appears.

Starting situation: 35–44 age band, employed, US. Work area: Retail operations.

Fictional finances: USD 1300 liquid funds; USD 2600 monthly income. Available time: 5 hours per week.

Constraint (soft): Prefer to keep a modest financial buffer while making progress.

## A different city, the same people

A possible move promises a change of scene, but there is no new job or home waiting. Exploring it means weighing money and unfamiliar routines against a valued relationship at home.

Goal: Explore a move to another city while maintaining a close friendship.

Motivation: Find a place and rhythm that fit without losing an important connection.

Starting situation: 35–44 age band, employed, US. Work area: Operations coordination.

Fictional finances: USD 4800 liquid funds; USD 2600 monthly income. Available time: 7 hours per week.

Constraint (soft): Prefer to keep a modest financial buffer while making progress.

## Making room for the unfinished work

Creative writing has been pushed aside by a regular working week. Returning to it starts with unfinished ideas, limited energy and the awkward prospect of sharing work before it feels ready.

Goal: Build a sustainable writing habit and complete a small collection of original short pieces.

Motivation: Recover a part of life that matters beyond earning a living.

Starting situation: 35–44 age band, employed, US. Work area: Administrative support.

Fictional finances: USD 3200 liquid funds; USD 2600 monthly income. Available time: 6 hours per week.

Constraint (soft): Prefer to keep a modest financial buffer while making progress.
